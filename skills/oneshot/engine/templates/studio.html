Motion Studio

Every film in this project, live from its source, in each of its formats, with its soundtrack. Nothing here is a video file: each film is drawn in the browser, frame by frame, from the same code that renders the MP4.

Space plays and pauses. The arrow keys step one frame. The page reads the films from films/. film.sh studio rebuilds them and reopens it.

To render an MP4: film.sh <film> preview (add wide for 16:9).
